Who We Are

Bonmas Gruppe is a vibrant, customer-centric informal retail distribution company dedicated to supplying essential goods and products to informal retailers and street vendors across Johannesburg. With a deep understanding of local market dynamics, we provide efficient, reliable, and convenient distribution solutions.

Our Mission

At Bonmas Gruppe, our mission is to make it easier for street vendors and informal retailers to access the products they need to grow their businesses. Through our own wholesale store, we provide a wide range of essential products under one roof, making sourcing easier, more affordable, and more efficient. We are also expanding into the bakery sector through our partnership with Krem Fine Bakers, allowing us to bring quality bakery products directly to informal retailers and street vendors. Our goal is to build a reliable supply network that empowers informal retailers, strengthens local businesses, and helps communities thrive.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading distribution and wholesale partner for informal retailers, creating a reliable supply network that enables entrepreneurs to access quality products, grow their businesses, and serve their communities more effectively. At Bonmas Gruppe, we believe that when informal retailers have better access to products, better supply solutions, and dependable business partners, local businesses thrive, communities grow, and opportunities are created.
